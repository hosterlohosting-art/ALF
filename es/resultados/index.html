We specialize in personal injury, car accidents, catastrophic injuries, y holding large insurance companies accountable for the harm they cause our clients.

You can schedule a consultation by calling our office directly, filling out the contact form below, or emailing us. Nuestro team is available 24/7.
¿Qué diferencia a Awad de otros bufetes de abogados?
We provide "excelente servicio". We treat every client like family, leave no stone unturned in our investigations, y prepare every single case as if it's going to trial.
