# Off-Page SEO Execution Plan

This file is the backlink, citation, and authority-building checklist for The Awad Law Firm. It is intentionally practical so the team can execute it without changing website code.

Use the exact same NAP everywhere:

- Business name: The Awad Law Firm
- Phone: (706) 388-3784
- Marietta office: 4076 Ebenezer Rd., Marietta, GA 30066
- Dalton office: 210 North Glenwood Ave., Dalton, GA 30721
- Website: https://theawadlawfirm.com/

Submit or update:

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Yelp
- Avvo
- FindLaw
- Justia
- Super Lawyers
- Lawyers.com
- Martindale
- HG.org
- Georgia Bar directory profile
- Local Chamber of Commerce listings for Marietta/Cobb County and Dalton/Whitfield County

## Priority 3: Review Growth

After every successful case close, ask for a review with a direct Google review link. Rotate language naturally and never offer compensation for reviews.

Suggested prompt:

> Thank you for trusting The Awad Law Firm. If our team helped you, your Google review helps other injured people find support when they need it most.

## Priority 4: Digital PR

Monthly pitches:

- Commentary on Georgia crash trends and distracted driving.
- Community event recaps with photos.
- Safety tips before holiday travel periods.
- Attorney Ibrahim Awad personal story, TEDx, and community advocacy.

Target outlets:

- Cobb County local news
- Dalton and Whitfield County publications
- Georgia legal blogs
- University/community newsletters
- Local podcast interviews

## Priority 5: Social Authority

Every new guide should be repurposed into:

- One short video
- One carousel
- One client-safe FAQ post
- One Google Business Profile update
- One email/newsletter section

Always link back to the matching canonical page on the website.
