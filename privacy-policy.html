Privacy Policy:
The Awad Law Firm

At The Awad Law Firm, we respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy
Official Document Last updated: May 31, 2026

The Awad Law Firm respects your privacy. Information you submit through this website, including contact forms, consultation requests, email links, and phone links, may be used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate a potential legal matter, and improve our client service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our services, when you contact us, or when you submit case details. This information may include:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, phone number, and physical mailing address.
  • Case-Related Information: Information about legal issues, incident descriptions, medical treatments, and other details you share when inquiring about representation.
  • Technical Data: Device and browser data, IP address, and basic analytics information about how visitors navigate and interact with our website.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect or receive to provide, improve, and secure our legal services and communications. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Communicate with you and respond to your consultation inquiries.
  • Schedule and manage personal injury consultations.
  • Review and evaluate potential legal claims for representation.
  • Maintain website security, verify safety, and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Improve our website, design system, articles, and legal resources for our visitors.

Note: Submitting an inquiry or case form through this website does not create an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship is only established through an executed written agreement signed by both parties.

3. Sharing of Information

The Awad Law Firm is dedicated to maintaining the absolute confidentiality of your legal inquiries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may only disclose or share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers and IT partners who assist in maintaining our website or secure case management platforms, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.
  • When required by law, subpoena, or standard judicial proceedings, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of our clients or the public.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement highly secure administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. While we employ industry-standard encryption and security measures, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure. Therefore, we encourage caution when transmitting extremely sensitive or confidential medical and legal records via public forms.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have choices regarding how we communicate with you and process your information:

  • You can opt out of receiving email newsletters or marketing updates from us by clicking the unsubscribe link in our mailings or contacting us directly.
  • You can request to update, correct, or delete the contact information you provided to us by sending an email request.
  • You can configure your browser settings to reject cookies or notify you when a cookie is placed, though some features of the website may function differently as a result.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to exercise any of your preferences, please contact our team at:
